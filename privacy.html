Text Message Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026 · North Point YSA Ward text list

Who we are

The North Point YSA Ward text list is run by the volunteer leaders of the North Point YSA Ward, a young single adult congregation of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in the Roswell Georgia Stake. This policy covers the information we collect when you join the text list.

What we collect

When you opt in we collect your mobile phone number, the name you give us, the date and method of your opt-in, and your consent. If you reply to a message, we keep your reply so a leader can respond.

How we use it

We use your number only to send the messages described in our Terms of Service — ward announcements, activity and event reminders, and Sunday information — and to respond when you reply. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties, except for the necessary opt-in data required to facilitate the SMS service. Messages are delivered by SimpleTexting, which processes your number solely to send our messages.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe at any time. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or service.

How long we keep it

Your number stays on the list until you reply STOP or ask us to remove it. You can ask us to delete your information at any time using the contact below.

Children

The text list is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update it here with a new effective date.
