Program description

The North Point YSA Ward text list sends informational text messages from the ward's leaders to members and friends of the North Point YSA Ward (Roswell Georgia Stake, The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints) who have opted in: ward announcements, activity and event reminders, and Sunday information. It is not a marketing program.

Opting in

You join by texting our number, by submitting our sign-up form, or by asking a ward leader to add you. By opting in you agree to receive recurring automated text messages, which may be sent using an autodialer, at the mobile number you provided. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or service.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies, up to 20 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan. We do not charge for the list.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You may receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed. After that you will receive no further messages unless you opt in again.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, or contact us at npysawardclerk@gmail.com.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Messages are delivered through SimpleTexting on our behalf.
